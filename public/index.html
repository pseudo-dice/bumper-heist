How to steal a win

  1. Drive with WASD, arrow keys, or the touch joystick.
  2. Bump with Space or the BUMP button. It recharges in 3 seconds.
  3. Even counts (2, 4, 6, 8) form two equal crews in Volt Stadium, assigned by join order. Odd counts (3, 5, 7) give each driver their own team in Orbit Yard. Both arenas grow with player count.
  4. Push the golden ball into your own or your crew’s colored goal for 1 shared point. Last touch doesn't matter.
  5. First to 5 wins, or highest score after 3 minutes of active play. Tied teams enter sudden death.
  6. Every 20 seconds: spinning bumpers, a crosswind, or extra-bouncy walls for 5 seconds.
  7. The dull ball marked ? is a decoy: it collides but never scores. Mint arrow pads launch carts and balls in their arrow direction.

Keep your tab open. If you disconnect, reconnect within 15 seconds; host control passes to another connected player after that.
